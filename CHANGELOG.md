# Changelog

## [2.1.0] — 2026-10-27

- Single unified, vendor-independent UDP specification with 50 articles.
- Consolidated evidence rules, honest adversarial review, existing-state checks, scope-bounded verdicts, and response auditing.
- Added proportional internal operating modes without separate protocol editions.
- Includes candidate evaluation scenarios and a conformance checklist; these are **not** experimental certifications.
- Initial public distribution package: Apache-2.0 `LICENSE`, attribution `NOTICE`, contribution guidance, and citation metadata.
- **Editorial publication correction:** Article 45.2 and Annex B's publication/license status are updated from their pre-publication placeholders to reflect the author's Apache-2.0 licensing decision. Original creator and publisher are identified. No deliberation or verification rule was changed by this licensing correction.

**Evidence status:** `SPECIFICATION_DRAFT` / `NOT_BENCHMARKED` / `NOT_CERTIFIED`.
